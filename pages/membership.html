How to join

  1. 1

    Be a member of AF

    You need to be a member of AF (Akademiska Föreningen) to join EASA. If you are a Studentlund member, you are automatically an AF member.

  2. 2

    Download & Sign-Up

    Download the ORBI app and create your account in connection with Lunds Universitet.

    Download the app
  3. 3

    Find & Apply

    Search for "EASA" in the app, go to "memberships" and select the option that works best for you. Fill out the required information

  4. 4

    Confirm & Join

    Submit your application and wait for approval. Once approved, you'll recieve a notification in the app. Complete your membership by confirming your purchase

How we handle your data

By submitting the membership form you consent to the processing of your personal data by the East Asian Student Association Lund (EASA Lund). The purpose of collecting this information is to enable EASA Lund to provide services related to your membership.

You have the legal right to, free of charge, access the data collected on you, to have erroneous data corrected, to have your data erased from our registries, to migrate your data to other systems through the use of widely available data formats, and to object to the use of your data for direct marketing, automated decision-making and profiling. If you wish to exercise these rights, or if you have any questions, please contact the secretary at president@asialund.org.

The personal data relating to your membership is deleted from the member registry by EASA Lund no later than two years after your membership has expired. EASA processes data in accordance with Article 6(1) of the Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation) and applicable Swedish law.

The personal data is also processed by, and stored on, third party services within and outside of the EU that comply with the GDPR. If you believe that your data is not processed according to applicable law, you can make a claim with the Swedish Data Protection Authority (Datainspektionen).

Being a member

Welcome to EASA! As a member, you can join many of our activities for free.

That covers all our East Asia-inspired events, from lectures and language exchange to social hangouts, movie nights, trips and sittningar. EASA's activities are open to everyone, and exist to bring together students with similar interests.

  • Social and cultural events

    Take part in our Asia-inspired events, such as Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn and our sittningar. Meet other students, join in challenges and games, and experience Asian food and culture.

  • Lectures

    We regularly invite interesting guests to give presentations on topics related to Asia, from politics and economics to history, society, technology and more.

  • Trips

    Join our trips across the world. We travelled to Japan in 2019, attended the Global Youth Trends Forum in Taiwan in 2019, and visited the Chinese and South Korean embassies in Stockholm in 2018. We plan trips regularly.

  • Board game and movie nights

    Watch an Asian movie together with other students, or learn how to play mahjong. Sit back, relax and have a great time.

Committees and roles

Auditor

You review the board's administration and accounts for the association's operational and fiscal year, and produce an audit report to present at the Annual Meeting.

Member of the election committee

The election committee is responsible for the election of the EASA Lund board. Its main job is to summon, interview and nominate candidates for all board positions before the Annual Election Meeting.

Member of the event committee

Interested in organizing socio-cultural events? You work closely with the Head of Events, planning, organizing and running fun Asian social and cultural events. The committee's purpose is to increase interest in and understanding of East and Southeast Asian culture, in line with the association's overall goal. Past examples include K-pop dance workshops, Lunar New Year, mahjong nights and movie nights.

Member of the lecture committee

Interested in sharing academic knowledge about East and Southeast Asia? You work closely with the Head of Lectures to plan, run, coordinate and moderate lectures, webinars, panel discussions and more, both online and on campus.

Member of the communication committee

Interested in content creation? You help manage EASA's social media, market lectures and events, create content, and get involved in graphic design and copywriting. Make EASA visible to a wider audience and grow engagement by running projects across different channels.

Travel coordinator

Interested in organizing trips with the association? You plan the timetable with support from the Head of External Relations. Tasks include booking tickets and accommodation, coordinating transport during the trip, and managing an itinerary of study visits and tours. Most important of all: keeping the group happy while learning, and making sure you are happy too.

Photographer

You take professional pictures of the board members and EASA activities.

Trusted positions

As a reward for being an active member who took on more responsibility and helped the association significantly, you can be elected to a trusted position. It gives access to board meetings and parts of the association's Google Drive. People in trusted positions can be given titles that fit their tasks, as agreed with the committee head, which can be valuable on your resume.
