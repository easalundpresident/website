Experiencing discrimination or harassment?

In EASA we do not tolerate any kind of discrimination or harassment.

If you have suffered harassment or discrimination, or you feel unsafe or worried, please reach out to any board member. EASA's contact persons for discrimination and sexual harassment have not been announced yet.

We also have an anti-harassment policy, to give members a positive atmosphere and protect them from a hostile or unpleasant environment. It guides us in making everyone feel safe and welcome, now and as we develop the association.
