About EASA

A platform for students interested in East and Southeast Asia, at Lund University.

Meet the board Open positions

Who we are

We promote academic and socio-cultural exchange between members from different backgrounds.

The East Asia Student Association Lund is a politically and religiously unaffiliated student association, recognized by the Academic Society (AF). We run a range of activities to bring people together.

We believe in human rights, equality, democracy and sustainability.

What we mean by East Asia

In the context of EASA, East Asia includes both East and Southeast Asia. We see it as containing:

  • People's Republic of China
  • Republic of China (Taiwan)
  • Hong Kong
  • Macau
  • Japan
  • Mongolia
  • Democratic People's Republic of Korea (North Korea)
  • Republic of Korea (South Korea)
  • Vietnam
  • Thailand
  • Laos
  • Cambodia
  • Myanmar
  • Malaysia
  • Indonesia
  • Singapore
  • Philippines
  • East Timor
  • Brunei
  • Andaman and Nicobar Islands
  • Cocos (Keeling) Islands

Why we exist

We see a great need for a platform for students interested in the East Asian region. Asia is growing in both economy and population, so it matters to spread knowledge of, and interest in, the region.

Lund University already has several programmes with an Asian focus:

  • MSc in East and Southeast Asian Studies
  • MSc in International Economics, with a focus on China
  • MSc in Chinese Language and Linguistics
  • MSc in Japanese Language and Linguistics

There are also many international and exchange students from East and Southeast Asia, and students with no academic or national link to Asia who are simply interested. All of them gain personally, academically and in their careers from knowing the region better and meeting people in its business and politics.

Board structure

EASA gives students at Lund University real, fun and useful work experience.

Board positions are applied for in May. Here is what each role does.

Roles

President

The president is responsible for the strategic work of the association. They plan and hold board meetings and delegate tasks to keep EASA moving forward. They lead and represent the association, sign contracts and act as a financial signatory.

Vice-President

The jack of all trades. The vice-president is the president's right hand, helps wherever things need doing, and steps in when the president is away.

Secretary

Keeps the board's documentation, such as meeting agendas and protocols, and does other administrative work. Also sends out periodic newsletters about EASA's updates and events, works strategically with the association, and can take on extra projects.

Treasurer

Together with the president and vice-president, handles the budget, receipts and other financial work. The treasurer is also a financial signatory.

Head of Events

Handles EASA's social and cultural activities, from smaller mingles and sittningar to events that build cultural awareness and understanding of East Asia. Makes sure members have fun while learning.

Head of Lectures

Organizes lectures and invites speakers, making sure EASA's lectures cover as many geographical and topical areas as possible. Collects lecture suggestions from active members, adds their own, and fills the calendar.

Head of External Relations

Manages professional contacts in Asia and relationships with key collaborators and partners. Also runs travel activities together with the Head of Grants.

Head of Grants

Oversees FBA-project activities, helps run travel activities with the travel coordinators and the Head of External Relations, and assists the president and treasurer with grant applications. Also finds new grants for the association to apply for. Most grant applications and collaborations are handled in Swedish, so the role suits a Swedish speaker, though that is not a requirement.

Head of Communication

Manages EASA's social media, marketing strategy and graphic design. Advises the board on optics and audience for events, works on the communications strategy and graphical profile, and makes sure events have a photographer.

Head of IT

Manages the website and the association's technical tasks, advising the team and finding and putting in place IT solutions. Works closely with the Head of Strategic Communication.
